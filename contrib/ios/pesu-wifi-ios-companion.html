⚙️ Zero-Click Personal Automation Setup

Configure iOS to run this shortcut automatically whenever your iPhone connects to campus Wi-Fi:

  1. Open the Shortcuts app on your iPhone → tap the Automation tab at the bottom.
  2. Tap New Automation (or + in the top right).
  3. Select Wi-Fi from the trigger list.
  4. Tap Choose Network → select PESU-RR-Campus, PESU-EC-Campus, and/or AMAATRA-HOSTEL.
  5. Select "Run Immediately". (Crucial: This ensures iOS authenticates silently without asking for manual confirmation).
  6. Tap Next → select PESU WiFi Login shortcut → Done!
💡 Captive Sheet Tip: The first time you connect to PESU Wi-Fi, if iOS pops up the captive login sheet, tap Cancel in the top-right corner, then tap "Use Without Internet". From then on, your Shortcut will authenticate silently in the background!
🛡️ Using a VPN on iOS? (Tailscale, WireGuard, WARP)

Full-tunnel VPNs route all device traffic into their tunnel. To allow campus portal authentication to succeed:

  • Tailscale: Open iOS Settings → Tailscale → Toggle ON "Allow Local Network Access".
  • WireGuard & WARP: In your tunnel settings, ensure private subnet 192.168.0.0/16 is excluded.
  • Shortcut VPN Action (Alternative): In the Shortcuts app, add a Set VPN → Disconnect action at the start, and Set VPN → Connect at the end.
